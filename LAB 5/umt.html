About Universiti Malaysia Terengganu

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public university located in Kuala Nerus District, Terengganu, Malaysia. It began as Universiti Pertanian Malaysia's Centre for Fisheries and Marine Science in Mengabang Telipot, providing facilities for students and lecturers to conduct practical sessions and research.

Eventually, the Faculty of Fisheries and Marine Science of Universiti Pertanian Malaysia in Serdang was transferred to Kuala Terengganu, and the branch campus was transformed into the Branch Campus of Universiti Putra Malaysia Terengganu (UPMT) in June 1996.

On 1 May 2001, it was upgraded to Kolej Universiti Sains dan Teknologi Malaysia (KUSTEM) before being officially renamed Universiti Malaysia Terengganu on 1 February 2007, focusing heavily on marine science and aquatic resources.

Key Faculties at UMT

Faculty Main Focus Areas
Faculty of Science and Marine Environment (FSSM) Marine Science, Marine Biology, Chemical Sciences
Faculty of Fisheries and Food Science (FSPA) Aquaculture, Fisheries, Food Technology
Faculty of Ocean Engineering Technology (FPEPS) Maritime Technology, Naval Architecture, Electrical Engineering
Faculty of Computer Science and Mathematics (FSKM) Software Engineering, Data Analytics, Artificial Intelligence, Mobile Computing
